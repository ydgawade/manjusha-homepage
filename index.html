About

An operating cadence teams can trust.

I help globally distributed product teams replace delivery ambiguity with a practical cadence: an actionable backlog, clear acceptance criteria, visible dependencies, evidence-based progress reporting, and ceremonies that lead to decisions.

My experience spans E-Commerce, inventory management, data and reporting, automotive repair technology, engineering quality and software delivery. I partner closely with Product Owners, business analysts, developers, QA, DevOps, vendors and business stakeholders across North America and the rest of the world.

With an engineering background and a delivery-lead mindset, I bring both the system view and the operational detail required to turn roadmaps into reliable outcomes.
