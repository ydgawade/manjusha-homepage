About

Delivery leadership with an engineer's rigor

I'm a technology leader in the engineering and automotive sector with a 14-year record of building high-performance solutions for customers across multiple industries. My work sits where strategy meets execution: evaluating and managing third-party developers and vendors, defining the best practices and processes teams actually use, and running several programs at once without letting quality, budget or timeline slip.

Earlier in my career I worked deep in product development and quality engineering — FMEA on engine controls, design verification, measurement system analysis, statistical process control. That foundation is why my agile coaching stays practical: I know what a real release risk looks like, and I know how to make a metric mean something.

I hold a Master of Science in Mechanical Engineering from Michigan Technological University and I'm certified as a PMP, Certified ScrumMaster and SAFe 6 Scrum Master.
